# Julien Gachadoat (v3ga)

- **Bio/context**: French generative artist and creative-coding educator based in Bordeaux; co-founded the interactive studio 2Roqs with Michaël Zancan and has taught creative coding for 15+ years. Works primarily in Processing/openFrameworks driving an AxiDraw pen plotter for physical ink drawings, alongside long-form on-chain generative work (Art Blocks, fxhash, Feral File). His lineage is explicitly the 1960s–70s computational pioneers (Vera Molnár, Herbert W. Franke); active plotter/NFT era ~2018–present.

- **Signature techniques**: Algorithmic geometric abstraction rendered as single-pen-width vector line art — sinusoidal line fields (`Sinusoïdes`), families of arcs/circles under incremental transformation, combinatorial grid systems that enumerate a rule-space (`Structures` = 256 states), and path/random-walk wandering (`Déambulations`). `DiceGL` (with Vera Molnár) drives composition from literal dice rolls; `Radiance`/`Haze` layer flow-field and noise-driven strokes. Tone is built entirely from line *density* (hatching, overlap) rather than fills.

- **Palette logic**: Predominantly monochrome — black ink on white paper, the native constraint of the plotter. Digital/NFT and silkscreen editions introduce restrained limited palettes (one or two accent inks, occasional CMYK-like separations); always high-contrast and reductive, never photographic gradients.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Crisp, uniform-weight vector strokes; flat and unmodeled. Density and Moiré emerge from overlapping regular line sets and offset arc families; the "texture" is purely the accumulation of clean lines, giving an engraved/technical-drawing feel.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: The plots are static but the underlying algorithm is fully parametric and step-able — for animation, drift the sine phase, rotate arc families incrementally, accumulate lines progressively (drawing-on), or morph combinatorially between grid states (section-to-section arc). Ideal for slow deterministic evolution and beat-synced state changes.

- **Key works**: *Structures* (256 combinatorial line compositions); *Radiance* (Art Blocks, 512, field-driven); *Haze* (tribute to Herbert W. Franke); *Umwelt*; *Mineral* (Feral File 1/1/30); *Sinusoïdes* (plotted sine fields); *Déambulations* (path/walk plotting); *Hyper* & *Pathways* (2018–23 pen-plotter book collection); *DiceGL* (dice protocol with Vera Molnár).
