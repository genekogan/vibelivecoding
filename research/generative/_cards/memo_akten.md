# Memo Akten

- **Bio/context**: British-Turkish computational artist (b. 1975, Istanbul; long based in London, later UC San Diego), PhD in Creative AI (Goldsmiths). Two eras: an openFrameworks/C++ + GLSL real-time period (~2007–2015) of fluids, particles and audio-reactive installation, then a deep-learning period (~2016–) of GAN latent-space video. Works span club visuals, laser/robotic-light installation, music videos (Depeche Mode) and gallery AI pieces.

- **Signature techniques**: (1) **MSAFluid** — his hugely influential port of Jos Stam's *Stable Fluids* (semi-Lagrangian advection + Gauss–Seidel pressure solve on a grid) with dye injection; velocity field is driven by hands/optical-flow/audio. (2) **GPU particle systems advected by that velocity field** (Body Paint, Forms) — hundreds of thousands of additive GL points/line-trails smeared along flow. (3) **Simple Harmonic Motion** — N sine oscillators with slightly detuned periods (a pendulum-wave); phase re-alignment creates travelling waves, mapped onto robotic lights, percussionists, plotters. (4) **Forms** (w/ Quayola) — motion-captured athletes drive splines, ribbons and polygonal accretion. (5) Deep-learning era: DCGAN/BigGAN/StyleGAN latent walks, and *Learning to See* = a pix2pix conditional GAN mapping a live webcam's edges to a trained image manifold (oceans, flowers, fire).

- **Palette logic**: mostly restrained, near-monochrome — Forms is desaturated graphite/steel on black; SHM is warm tungsten light on black; the GAN pieces inherit the training set's palette (oceanic blues, floral, ember-fire). Additive light on black is the through-line, with sparse single accents.

- **Mark-making / texture**: additive-blended thin points and long motion-blur strokes that sum into volumetric glow and HDR bloom; in the GAN work, painterly hallucinated impasto. Rarely flat — almost everything is luminous accumulation.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: fluid advection drift; SHM's slow re-phasing travelling waves with beat-locked accents; audio-reactive onset/amplitude driving particle emission and fluid forces; and in the AI work, continuous slow latent-space morphing between learned images.

- **Key works**: *MSAFluid* (2008, open-source addon); *Body Paint* (2009); *Reincarnation* (2009); *Forms* (2012, w/ Davide Quayola); *Simple Harmonic Motion* (2011–, esp. #12 for 16 percussionists); *Learning to See* (2017); *Deep Meditations* (2018).
