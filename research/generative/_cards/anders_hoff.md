# Anders Hoff (inconvergent)

- **Bio/context**: Norwegian generative artist and mathematician; makes and sells generative **pen-plotter** ink drawings and publishes deeply-documented algorithm essays at inconvergent.net. Works primarily in **Python and Common Lisp**, releasing his own libraries (**snek**, **lin**, **weir**, fractal utilities) rather than off-the-shelf frameworks. A canonical creative-coding reference — his differential-growth and hyphae write-ups seeded countless reimplementations.
- **Signature techniques**: **Differential line growth** — a polyline of nodes where each node feels short-range repulsion from neighbors plus attraction along the path, with new nodes inserted whenever an edge stretches past a threshold, so the line buckles and folds into brain-coral convolutions. **Hyphae** — space-colonization / non-overlapping circle growth spawning root/vein networks (his recreation of Nervous System's Hyphae). **Differential mesh** (2D/3D lichen-like sheet growth). **Sandpaint** — his signature rendering: accumulate millions of translucent points to build continuous tonal density and grain. Plus **fractures**, **sand splines**, and orbital/attractor line fields.
- **Palette logic**: Near-monochrome and austere — overwhelmingly **black ink on white** (or inverted white/pale line on dark), with density itself carrying tone; occasional single ink accent (his shop uses named inks) but essentially achromatic, letting structure and line-density do the work.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Fine hairline strokes and dense **sandpaint** grain — the image is built from countless faint points/lines so gradients emerge as stippled, dusty accumulation. Delicate, high-line-count, plotter-precise; flat (no shading), with texture entirely from mark density.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: The *process* is the artwork — iterative growth simulations that evolve slowly frame-to-frame (lines subdividing and buckling, veins colonizing space, grains accreting) until the field saturates. Reads as patient organic spreading/branching; the ideal VJ analogue is a slow differential-growth crawl or hyphae vein-colonization advancing across the canvas.
- **Key works**: *Hyphae* (vein/root networks); *Differential Growth* / *Differential Mesh* (buckling coral lines, lichen sheets); *Fractures*; *Sand Spline* & *sandpaint* studies; *Orbitals*; the ongoing plotter-drawings series (img.inconvergent.net/plot).
