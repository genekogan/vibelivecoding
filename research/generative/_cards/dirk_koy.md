# Dirk Koy

- **Bio/context**: Basel-based experimental filmmaker & motion-graphics artist (b. 1977), HGK Basel (Visual Communication), lecturer in Time-Based Media there since 2011; studio *Dirk Koy Bild und Bewegung* (2016). Basel Media Art Prize 2019, Prix Ars Electronica honorary mention (*The City*, 2012). Works primarily in **short looping moving image** (Vimeo/Instagram) and **NFT editions** (SuperRare, Verse, Le Random). Tooling is the commercial motion pipeline — **Cinema 4D, After Effects, photogrammetry (RealityCapture/Metashape), drones** — not p5, but the *looks* are prime VJ material.

- **Signature techniques**: **Photogrammetry / 3D-scan point clouds** of real places and bodies, then **deliberately broken** — meshes torn, displaced, and re-flowed. **Digital "faults" as a visual language**: datamosh, pixel-smear, RGB/displacement glitch, morph-target interpolation, and **fluid/jelly deformation** where solid architecture or a face wobbles like liquid. Heavy use of **displacement maps and noise-driven morphing** to melt one state into another, and **drone orthographic top-downs** of landscape re-rendered as flowing texture. Motifs: waves/water, flowers, portraits, urban facades — each liquefied or shattered.

- **Palette logic**: Punchy and often synthetic — clean **high-key whites and pastel gradients** (sky-blue → pink) or **bold saturated primaries** on flat grounds; sometimes near-monochrome scan-grey with a single acid accent. Frequent **iridescent / chrome / oil-slick** shading on the morphing surfaces. Crisp digital contrast, poster-like flat backgrounds behind glossy or point-sampled subjects.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Two textures in tension — **dense point-cloud stipple/particulate** (scan dots, sparkling grain) versus **smooth liquid-plastic morphing surfaces**. Plus glitch artifacts: block smear, scanline tear, chromatic fringing. Clean, graphic-design-trained framing; centered subjects; lots of empty flat negative space.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: This is the core — **short seamless loops** built on **continuous morph and advection**: architecture ripples like cloth, water/flowers bloom and reset, point clouds swirl and re-coalesce into a recognizable form then dissolve. Motion is **fluid, hypnotic, elastic**, often with a subtle beat/pulse and a satisfying loop point. For a VJ engine: displacement-map wobble on an image/mesh, point-cloud scatter-and-gather, datamosh smear pulses on transients.

- **Key works**: *The City* (2012, Ars Electronica mention); *Bildraum* & *Salvage* (Le Random collection, glitched spatial scans); *Voicemail* (SHOWstudio); *Waves*, *Flowers*, *Faces/Portraits* loop series; assorted SuperRare/Verse morphing-architecture editions.
