# Casey Reas

- **Bio/context**: American artist (b. 1972, Ohio; based in Los Angeles), **co-creator of Processing** (with Ben Fry, 2001) and a UCLA Design Media Arts professor. A foundational figure bridging conceptual art (Sol LeWitt's instruction drawings) and software art. Medium: custom software (Processing/Java, later GLSL/video), prints, and installations; represented by **bitforms gallery**. Era 2001–present.
- **Signature techniques**: **Conditional / instruction-based systems.** *Software Structures* (2004, Whitney) implements written instructions à la LeWitt. The **Process series** (2005–) defines a "Process" as an environment of many autonomous **Elements** — soft-circle agents each obeying simple behaviors (move, avoid, connect, follow) — whose overlapping trails and proximity-connections accumulate into emergent tonal fields (close kin to flocking + proximity-graph drawing). Later bodies of work invert the logic toward signal: *Signal to Noise / KNBC* **feedback-process broadcast TV**, and *Century / Ultraconcentrated* apply **GLSL manipulation and RGB video feedback** to scanned printed matter.
- **Palette logic**: Two poles. The **Process/Tissue** works are austere — grayscale, black-on-white, or a single red/ochre accent, foregrounding structure over color. The **signal/video** works (*Signal to Noise*, *Century*, *Ultraconcentrated*) explode into saturated RGB: chromatic feedback smears, TV-phosphor red/green/blue, glitch color.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Process works are built from **thin translucent lines and soft discs over-plotted into gauzy "tissue"** — delicate, drawing-like tonal accumulation. The video works are the opposite: **scanline smear, blur, RGB channel bleed, analog-signal noise** — dense electronic haze.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Most pieces are **live software running continuously and never exactly repeating** — the Processes drift and rearrange indefinitely (slow, organic, non-looping), while *Signal to Noise* flickers and tears with broadcast rhythm. For a VJ engine, two directly usable modes: (1) agent fields that slowly accumulate and re-form without repeating; (2) a "video-feedback" mode with RGB smear/decay.
- **Key works**: *Process 4–18* (agent systems); *Tissue* (line-connection drawings); *Software Structures* (2004); *MicroImage*; *Signal to Noise / KNBC* (2007, TV feedback); *Century* (2012); *Ultraconcentrated*; *A Mathematical Theory of Communication* (2016, UT Austin).
