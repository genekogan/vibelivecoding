# Etienne Jacob (bleuje)

- **Bio/context**: French generative artist and mathematics teacher (Rennes), known for short, **perfectly seamless looping animations** — mostly black-and-white — made in **Processing**. Huge following on Instagram/Tumblr (`necessary-disorder`) and X; author of the widely-cited seamless-looping tutorials that taught a generation the technique. Era ~2016–present.
- **Signature techniques**: The core idea is **time as a looping parameter**: a global `t ∈ [0,1)` maps to angle `2πt`, and every animated quantity is a **periodic function of t**, so the last frame equals the first (a truly seamless GIF). He propagates one periodic motion **across space with a spatial phase offset** (delay by distance/index) → traveling waves, ripples, breathing lattices. Toolkit: **cyclic noise** (sample Perlin noise on a *circle* in noise-space so the randomness itself loops), particle systems on trigonometric paths, **metaballs / isovalue contouring**, distance-field patterns, custom easing/gain functions, and a **motion-blur template** (adapted from beesandbombs) for buttery frames.
- **Palette logic**: Predominantly high-contrast **monochrome** — white forms on black (or inverted), grayscale gradients. When color appears it's restrained: a single hue ramp or a duotone. The work is about motion and form, not color.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Clean geometric primitives — dot grids, small circles/spheres, lines, filled contours — with crisp anti-aliased edges, subtle **motion blur**, and occasional soft radial shading that fakes 3D relief. Flat backgrounds; no grain.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: This *is* the work — hypnotic, mechanical-yet-organic loops of a few seconds where **phase-offset waves sweep across grids**, particles orbit and re-converge, and contours pulse/invert, everything easing smoothly and returning exactly to start. For a VJ engine this is the master recipe: make any field loop perfectly by expressing it in periodic-`t`, and give it travel by offsetting phase by position.
- **Key works**: his numbered daily loops (`necessary-disorder` Tumblr / Instagram `@bleuje`); tutorials *"Noise loop / noise propagation"*, *"Making animations with a time variable"*, *"Motion blur template"*; recurring pieces — dot-grid traveling waves, metaball blobs, particle-convergence loops; fxhash generative loops.
