# Yugo Nakamura

- **Bio/context**: Japanese creative director, designer and engineer (b. 1970, Nara); trained in **civil engineering, architecture and landscape** before becoming a defining figure of the **Flash/ActionScript** golden age (late 1990s–2000s). Ran studio **tha ltd.**, built FFFFOUND!, UNIQLOCK, and later the video game *HUMANITY* (2023) and FRAMED* digital-art works. His credo: model interface behavior on the **mathematics of natural complexity** so it feels alive.
- **Signature techniques**: **physics-based interaction** — spring–mass–damper systems, easing/inertia, overshoot and damped oscillation applied to UI, typography and grids. Cursor-following elements lag and settle; **grids of cells deform and ripple** in response to input (e.g. *Nervous Matrix*: a 3×3 Mona-Lisa grid whose cells resize on keys 1–9). Generative **time-driven loops** (clocks, synchronized motion). Interactive kinetic typography where letters behave like sprung physical objects.
- **Palette logic**: restrained, near-**Swiss/Japanese minimalism** — predominantly black-on-white or greyscale with generous negative space; sparing, punchy accent color (a single red/blue/yellow pop). Color is functional and quiet, subordinate to motion.
- **Mark-making / texture**: crisp flat **vector UI** — thin lines, dots, small glyphs, tick-marks and grids; no texture or grain; precise, weightless, diagrammatic. The "material" is behavior, not surface.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: **the point of the work.** Everything moves with **elastic, damped, physical inertia** — spring overshoot, settle, ripple propagation across a lattice, cursor lag. Time-based generative loops tick and pulse (UNIQLOCK's dancers synced to the system clock; IndustriousClock's typographic seconds). Motion is continuous, organic, and reactive rather than cut/keyframed.
- **Key works**: *MONO*crafts* (1999–2000, seminal interactive Flash portfolio); *Nervous Matrix* (interactive deforming image grid); *FractalHK*; *IndustriousClock* (kinetic typographic clock); *Ecotonoha* (2003, collective tree-drawing eco-artwork); *UNIQLOCK* (2007, time-synced dance/clock, D&AD/Cannes-winning); *Mikaball*; FRAMED* pieces (*JamPack*, *Border*); art-direction of *HUMANITY* (2023).
