# Eno Henze

- **Bio/context**: German artist, scenographer and designer (b. 1977), based between Berlin and Frankfurt; active in the **vvvv / NODE Forum** digital-arts community. His work interrogates man–machine cooperation — images and spaces that "oscillate between great complexity and clarity, between human and machine origin." Medium: **algorithmically generated vector line-drawings** output at large scale (plotter/print), plus installations and performances. Shown at the Moscow Biennale, Art Cologne, Frankfurter Kunstverein.
- **Signature techniques**: **generative clouds of thousands of straight vector line-segments**. A stochastic process scatters short lines with controlled position/orientation distributions; local **density gradients** accrete into volumetric "clouds," explosion cores, shrapnel bursts and mirror-symmetric masses. Recurring language of **aggression / attack aesthetics** — the marks read as detonations, ambushes, event-horizons, Cherenkov radiation. Some pieces impose **radial/bilateral symmetry** on the segment field; others let it disperse into foam.
- **Palette logic**: overwhelmingly **monochrome — dense black line on white** (ink/plotter register), maximizing graphic contrast; occasional single saturated accent (notably **red**, e.g. *Red Ambush*). Tone is produced entirely by line *density*, not fill — sparse regions read light, packed regions read black.
- **Mark-making / texture**: crisp, thin, mechanical **vector strokes** with no grain or modeling; the aggregate texture is a **hatched/streaked cloud** whose value is pure accumulation. Sharp-edged, flat, high-frequency — closer to engraving or radiography than painting.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: for an animated port, the natural time-behavior is **accretion and dispersal** — segments streaming in to build a dense core then exploding/thinning out; density fields pulsing; symmetry axes rotating. Think emergent swarm → detonation → decay arcs.
- **Key works** (titles from his catalog): *Red Ambush*; *Subjektbeschleuniger* ("subject accelerator"); *Ereignishorizont II* ("event horizon"); *Große Symmetrie* ("grand symmetry"); *Schaum* ("foam"); *Tscherenkov* (Cherenkov-radiation motif); *Wesung* — all dense black line-cloud drawings in the "aesthetics of aggression" vein.
