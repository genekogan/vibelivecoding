# Holger Lippmann

- **Bio/context**: German artist (b. 1960, Colditz), based in Berlin; trained as a painter (HfBK Dresden / studies in Paris and New York) before moving through electronic music into code. A pioneer of net.art who has branded his practice "e-art"; ~25 years of generative work realized almost entirely in **Processing (Java)**, output as scalable vector/raster stills and animations, and since 2021 released as on-chain editions on **Art Blocks** and **fxhash**.
- **Signature techniques**: Perlin/Simplex **noise fields** are the backbone — used as heightfields, flow fields, and color-index fields. Recurring methods: noise-driven **flow-field advection** (blobs/contours dragged horizontally leaving layered trails, as in WaVyScApE), **recursive/L-system tree and flower** structures, **gradient-mesh color-field** layering, and a "code-minimalist" geometric vocabulary of only circles, squares and lines (grid pieces). Motifs cluster into four lifelong themes he names himself: Land/Wave, Grid/Structure, Circle/Round, Tree/Flower.
- **Palette logic**: lush, painterly, often high-chroma gradients built from **noise-sampled random color sequences** blended smoothly across the field; frequently overlaid/knocked-back with white and greyscale washes for atmospheric depth; ranges from psychedelic rainbow landscapes to restrained near-monochrome constructivist grids. Color is treated like a painter's glaze — layered, translucent, smoothly interpolated rather than flat-filled.
- **Mark-making / texture**: vector-clean and resolution-independent — smooth gradient fills, soft translucent trails, and subtle directional shading that fakes a **pseudo-3D relief** (rolling-hill / cloud-bank illusion). Little to no grain; the surface reads as digital-painterly, flat-to-modeled, with crisp geometric edges in the grid work.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: slow, meditative **drift and morph**. WaVyScApE's forms migrate horizontally while trailing history behind them; other works breathe via slowly evolving noise-time (`z` axis of 3D noise as a clock). Think gentle advection, gradual color-phase cycling, and continuous non-looping evolution rather than beats or cuts.
- **Key works**: *WaVyScApE* (Art Blocks Curated, 2023, 315 outputs — noise-turbulence "linear landscapes"); *NEBELWALD* & *SCHNEEWALD* (fxhash 2023, seasonal fog/snow forests); *Neo-Geometrische-Kunst* (1996–2002, hard-edge constructivist grids); *Minimal Garden* (1999–2001, early web-generative); *DATA-DONUT* (concentric ring fields, inspired by DOS defrag UIs); *noiseGrid* (2014); *Unfolding Silently*; plus *e-pastel* (a standalone generative-pastel program).
