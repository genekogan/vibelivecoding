# Marcin Ignac

- **Bio/context**: Polish data artist and computational designer in London; founder of studio **Variable.io** and co-creator of the node-based generative tool **Nodes**. Background in computer science, new-media art and interaction design. Author of the WebGL libraries **pex** and **regl** ecosystem contributions; works span data visualization, generative 3D, plotter drawings and fxhash mints, for clients like Nike, Google, Twitter, Monotype and Wellcome Trust.

- **Signature techniques**: **Procedural organic form** via parametric surfaces of revolution and marching-cubes iso-surfaces (Cindermedusae's jellyfish), **reaction-diffusion**-textured creatures (Seadragons, patterns then displaced onto 3D and 3D-printed), agent/L-system-style **generative growth** (Urban Flora plants; procedural cities in Algorithmic Memory), and large-scale **data-driven visualization** (shipping/flight flows, force-directed graphs, geospatial arcs). Heavy WebGL/GLSL, isosurface extraction, and custom design tools to explore a possibility space.

- **Palette logic**: Naturalist/scientific — inspired by Ernst Haeckel's *Kunstformen der Natur* lithographs: luminous translucent forms on deep near-black grounds, subtle iridescent membranes, restrained earthy or bioluminescent accents. Data pieces use clean analytic ramps (sequential/diverging) rather than decorative color.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Modeled, volumetric 3D with soft subsurface/translucency and fine surface detail from reaction-diffusion or noise displacement; also crisp vector line for plotter and data work. Ranges from delicate x-ray/wireframe debug aesthetics to fully shaded organic renders.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Forms **grow, morph and breathe** — parameters interpolate to make jellyfish pulse and tentacles undulate, RD patterns diffuse over time, plants germinate and branch, cities accrete. Data artworks "grow, change and transform over time, responding to changes in data." Slow, life-like drift and rotation; excellent reference for evolving creature/parametric scenes.

- **Key works**: *Cindermedusae* (algorithmic Haeckel-style jellyfish, featured in writtenimages.net); *Seadragons* (reaction-diffusion creatures, 3D-printed); *Urban Flora* (generative plants, fxhash); *Algorithmic Memory* (procedural cities/memory); *Inflows/Outflows & Sea Transport Network* (shipping-data viz); *Structures of Radiolaria*.
