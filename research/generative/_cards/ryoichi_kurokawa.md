# Ryoichi Kurokawa

- **Bio/context**: Japanese audiovisual artist (b. 1978, Osaka; based Berlin). Works across single-channel video, **multi-screen sculptural installations**, and live AV performance. Won the **Golden Nica, Prix Ars Electronica 2010** for *rheo: 5 horizons*. Active mid-2000s–present. Fuses image and sound into a single, sample-tight synchronized organism.

- **Signature techniques**: **3D laser scanning / LiDAR + photogrammetry** of real architecture, ruins and nature → dense **point clouds** that he distorts, fragments and re-assembles into modular **"subassemblies"** via custom shading language ("scan real elements, then transform the point-cloud data with shading language"). **Datamosh / glitch**: nature footage shattered into blocks, smeared and torn. Particle systems and point-cloud rendering where shading reveals or dissolves structure. **Extreme audio-visual sync** — every visual cut/glitch has a matching audio transient. Multi-panel **spatial montage** across 5+ HD screens. Scientific data as source (astrophysics for *unfold*; CERN scans for *subassemblies*).

- **Palette logic**: Mostly **desaturated, naturalistic-to-monochrome** — dark fields with high-contrast white/gray point structures, muted earth tones pulled from scanned nature, and selective cold **cyan/blue** or warm **ember** accents; iridescent shimmer on waveform passages. Restrained and near-grayscale, never candy-colored.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Fine **point-cloud stipple**, blocky **glitch/datamosh** artifacts, torn video slices, wireframe/edge structures, crisp geometric slabs colliding with organic scans, film-grain darkness, and additive sparkle on particle bursts.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: "Incessant energy flow" modeled on nature — landscapes **burst into static/waveforms and re-coalesce**; rapid stutter-cuts, freeze-and-shatter, seismic jolts; slow drifting camera moves through point-cloud ruins punctuated by violent glitch spasms, all beat-locked to the sound. The core arc is **fragmentation → reassembly**.

- **Key works**: **rheo: 5 horizons** (2009–10, five vertical screens, glitch landscapes) · **sirens** (2011) · **ad/ab Atom** (2012–13, monolithic AV sculpture) · **unfold** (2016, star-formation/astrophysics; 3ch projection + tactile transducers) · **node** (2018, 10-node point-cloud sculpture) · **subassemblies** (2018–, laser-scanned architecture + CERN data) · **octfalls** · **elementum**.
