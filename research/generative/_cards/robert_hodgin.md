# Robert Hodgin (flight404)

- **Bio/context**: American artist (RISD sculpture background), Brooklyn-based; blogged as **flight404** through the 2000s and co-created the **Cinder** C++ creative-coding framework (with Andrew Bell & Hai Nguyen). Co-founder / Head of R&D at studio **Rare Volume**. Works in real-time GPU C++ (Cinder; formerly Processing), spanning particle physics, flocking, terrain and astronomical simulation.
- **Signature techniques**: Physically-based **particle systems** with opposing attract/repel forces (Magnetosphere). **Boids flocking** (Reynolds separation/alignment/cohesion) scaled to tens of thousands of agents on the GPU — Murmuration (~40k starlings), Fish Tornado, and shark/fish predator–prey schooling. **Procedural river morphodynamics**: spline channels that migrate outward on the outer bank, self-intersect and pinch off **oxbow lakes**, laying down floodplain scars (Meander / River Scars). N-body / orbital-mechanics astronomical viz (Planetary, Solar, Star Chart). Heavy use of audio-FFT reactivity.
- **Palette logic**: Dark-key throughout — deep black/navy voids with **additive-blended glowing particles**; iridescent, spectral color transitions (hue shifted by velocity, audio band, or field angle) reading as bioluminescent/plasma. Meander switches to cartographic false-color (ribboned age-of-channel gradients).
- **Mark-making / texture**: Luminous points, streaks and motion-trails on dark grounds; additive glow and bloom, subtle depth-of-field and volumetric haze. Modeled 3D shading on organisms/terrain vs. pure additive light for particle fields.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Everything is a live simulation — swirling attract/repel breathing to a beat (audio-reactive), murmuration flocks folding and rippling, rivers slowly migrating over "years." Motion IS the work: emergent, organic, continuously evolving, frequently sound-driven. Prime VJ source — Magnetosphere literally shipped as the default iTunes 8 visualizer (2008).
- **Key works**: *Magnetosphere* (attract/repel particle audio-visualizer → default iTunes 8 visualizer); *Meander* / *River Scars* (procedural meandering-river maps with oxbows); *Murmuration* (40k GPU starlings); *Fish Tornado* & *Moana My Ocean* (schooling / feeding-frenzy sims); *Planetary* (iPad music-as-solar-systems); *Solar* / *Star Chart* (astronomical fields).
