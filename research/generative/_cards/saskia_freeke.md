# Saskia Freeke

- **Bio/context**: Dutch artist, visual/interaction designer, creative coder and educator based in London (handle "Sasj"). Best known for an unbroken daily-art practice begun 1 January 2015 — thousands of small looping geometric animations. Primary medium is **Processing + the HYPE framework**; she also drafts shapes in Hexels, exports to SVG, and re-composes them in code, and performs live visuals with **Hydra**. Teaches creative coding (Goldsmiths / workshops).

- **Signature techniques**: Grid-based geometric systems animated with trigonometry. The core recipe is a regular lattice of primitives (circles, triangles, rectangles, arcs, short lines) where each cell's rotation, scale, position or color is driven by `sin`/`cos` with a **phase offset keyed to grid index** (`phase = frameCount*speed + (x+y)*k`), producing travelling waves, ripples, blinking cascades and breathing across the field. Also radial/phyllotaxis arrangements, modular tiling, mirrored symmetry, and eased state-to-state transitions. She works in **weekly themes**, iterating one constraint for seven days.

- **Palette logic**: Bright, cheerful and harmonious — flat color-blocking with a curated 3–6 hue palette per piece, mixing bold saturated tones with softer pastels, usually on a warm cream/off-white or a single flat background. Retro/graphic sensibility; color is often assigned per-cell so the palette itself animates (hue cycling across the grid).

- **Mark-making / texture**: Flat vector fills with hard, precise edges and no grain or gradient shading. Clean and graphic — occasional outlined/stroked shapes or dotted fills, but always crisp geometric primitives; the richness comes from repetition and phase, not surface texture.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Short, **seamlessly looping** GIFs (a few seconds). Metronomic and hypnotic — grid-wide phase-offset oscillation yields traveling ripples, synchronized/desynchronized blinking, rotation waves, and gentle "breathing" scale pulses. Motion is smooth and eased, endlessly repeatable, satisfying like a mechanism.

- **Key works**: *Daily Art* (2015–present, the ongoing corpus); *Geometric Animations* series (e.g. "161016"); annual daily-art compilation videos; weekly-theme sets exploring one motif at a time.
