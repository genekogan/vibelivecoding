# Jared Tarbell

- **Bio/context**: American generative artist and programmer (b. 1973, Albuquerque NM); ran the seminal "gallery of computation" sites levitated.net (2001, Flash/ActionScript) and complexification.net (2003–05, Processing/Java), releasing nearly every piece as open source. Co-founded Etsy (2005) and wrote much of its early code; later moved toward laser-cut and plotter physical work. A foundational figure of the Processing-era "complexity from simple rules" school.
- **Signature techniques**: Agent/particle "drawing machines" governed by tiny local rules. **Substrate**: crack-propagation — line agents march forward and spawn perpendicular children on collision (city-like crazing), each crack flood-filled by a watercolor sand-painter. **Sand Painter** (his signature fill): thousands of very-low-alpha points jittered along a path with per-grain color drift, building sedimentary grain instead of solid fills. **Force-directed graphs** (Happy Place, Node Garden) relaxing node–node springs + proximity links. **Bubble Chamber** particle physics with decaying spiral trails. **Invader Fractal** recursive IFS tiling of pixel-invader motifs. Also Henon/Clifford attractors.
- **Palette logic**: Restrained, sampled palettes — small hand-picked swatch sets (often lifted from photographs or earthy "desert" tones: ochre, rust, slate, cream) that each sand-painter grain drifts slightly around, giving muted, faded-print harmonies rather than saturated primaries. White or near-black grounds.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Hairline 1px translucent strokes and pointillist grain accumulation; overlapping low-alpha marks integrate into soft, sedimentary, almost watercolor/sandstone texture. Flat (no shading, no 3D) — depth comes purely from density of accumulated marks.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Pieces are *accretive* — they start sparse and grow, agents crawling and depositing until the frame silts up and reaches equilibrium, then reset. Slow crystalline spread (Substrate), orbital drift (Sand Traveler), network settling (Happy Place). Evolution is monotonic build-up rather than a loop — for VJ use, run a growth cycle then dissolve/reseed.
- **Key works**: *Substrate* (crack-growth cityscape, his most famous piece); *Sand Traveler* / *Sand Dollar* (orbiting sand-painter particle rings); *Happy Place* (force-directed friend graph relaxing into clusters); *Node Garden* (proximity-linked point field); *Bubble Chamber* (decaying particle trails); *Invader Fractal* (recursive IFS invaders); plus *Binary Ring*, *Guts*, *Tree Garden*.
- **Links**: complexification.net (gallery + source), levitated.net, jaredstarbell.com (current work); interviews at artnome.com and katevassgalerie.com.
