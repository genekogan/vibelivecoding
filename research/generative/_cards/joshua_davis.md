# Joshua Davis

- **Bio/context**: American designer/technologist/artist (b. 1971); creator of **praystation.com** (Flash/ActionScript; Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica, 2001) and a pioneer of open-source generative design. Co-author of the **HYPE framework** (a high-level Processing/Java library for generative art). Splits commercial work (Nike, BMW, Barneys, motion/branding) and fine art; process = code-driven composition of hand-drawn vector assets, often "painting with sound" (coding sketches while listening to music).
- **Signature techniques**: **Generative collage / parametric layout** — a library of hand-illustrated vector modules (organic blobs, botanical flourishes, skulls, ornaments) instanced, scaled, rotated and recombined by controlled randomness over grids, circle-packings, polar arrays and sphere projections (HYPE's `HDrawablePool` populating a canvas). Uses **weighted probability tables** to choose assets and **color-spectrum / gradient-map sampling** to drive palette; layered with drop-shadows for depth.
- **Palette logic**: Bold, high-chroma, maximalist — colors pulled by sampling along authored gradient ramps / spectrum bitmaps so an entire composition stays in a coherent but vivid family (acid brights, jewel tones, or tuned duotone runs). Frequent stark black-and-white "praystation" studies as the austere counterpoint.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Crisp flat vector shapes with clean outlines and soft drop-shadows; dense decorative layering (ornament stacked on ornament) yields busy, poster-like, screenprint-flavored surfaces. Flat fills, not modeled — richness comes from quantity and overlap.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Compositions regenerate — each run reshuffles module placement/scale/color into a new arrangement (poster-generator feel). Animated versions oscillate, swarm and drift assets using HYPE's oscillation/hit-detection behaviors. Reads as evolving kaleidoscopic wallpaper rather than a physics sim — for VJ use, continuously re-instance and cross-fade layouts.
- **Key works**: *praystation* (open-source Flash experiments); *Once Upon A Forest* (lyrical vector-botanical generative series); *Dreamless*; the **HYPE framework** (tool + aesthetic); *Generative / Generate* print series; commercial identity/motion (Nike, BMW, MTV, Barneys window installs).
