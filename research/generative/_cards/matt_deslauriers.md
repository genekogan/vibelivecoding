# Matt DesLauriers

- **Bio/context**: Canadian-born generative artist and creative coder based in London; a pillar of the open-source creative-coding ecosystem (author of `canvas-sketch`, plus many glsl/color/geometry npm modules). Works in JavaScript/canvas/WebGL with deterministic hash→image pipelines. Best known for the long-form Art Blocks releases *Subscapes* (2021) and *Meridian* (2021–22).

- **Signature techniques**: *Meridian* is a ~15kb JS program that constructs **stratified landforms from hundreds of thousands of tiny colored strokes**, simulating layered ink-pen terrain (often only 2–3 "pens"). *Subscapes* draws parametric landscape lines/forms deterministically from a token hash. Across the work: flow/noise-field-driven stroke advection, Poisson-disk sampling, graph/spanning-tree sketches, dithering and grain, and GLSL shaders — all seeded by a coordinate in a high-dimensional "generative space."

- **Palette logic**: Carefully curated limited palettes, frequently reading as 2–3 real ink colors as if drawn with a pen set; elsewhere, rich naturalistic terrain gradients. Strong risograph/print sensibility — harmonies pulled from hand-curated color lists, tuned per output.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Painterly accumulation of hundreds of thousands of small brush/pen strokes that follow terrain contours, building stratified, sedimentary texture with grain, stippling, and simulated ink bleed; layered translucency rather than flat fills — "marks inconceivable in analog media."

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: For animation, drive the **stroke-by-stroke accumulation** (progressive draw reveal), advect strokes along the noise/flow field, slowly traverse the multidimensional parameter space so terrain and palette drift continuously, and cycle the limited palette. Naturally suits slow, meditative, landscape-like arcs.

- **Key works**: *Meridian* (Art Blocks, 1000 stratified landscapes); *Subscapes* (Art Blocks, 650 parametric terrains); *FOLIO*; *Pattern Language* (2024, generative Åkle weaving); *The Sferic Project* (2023–, Earth radio/atmospheric noise); *Wayfinder* (generative poetry game); *ALICE*.
