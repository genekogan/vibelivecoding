# Dextro (Walter Gorgosilits)

- **Bio/context**: Austrian net-art and algorithmic pioneer, born 1968 in Mödling, studied graphic design in Vienna. Runs dextro.org since 1994 (~450 works), one of the earliest net-art projects. Co-created turux.org with LIA in the mid-90s (interactive Flash/Director "semi-automatic" pieces); since 2002 shifted to high-resolution, non-real-time-rendered algorithmic videos as his scripts outgrew real-time playback.

- **Signature techniques**: Hand-written **non-linear code, explicitly "not fractal, not random"** — deterministic organic systems programmed from scratch. Boid-like particle figurations and bespoke per-pixel display produce abstract forms analogous to waves, sand dunes, cells, membranes, and frequencies; self-organization is the governing idea. The turux collaboration was "semi-automatic": interactive within a predefined frame of possible behaviors.

- **Palette logic**: Wide range — from high-key saturated spectral washes and psychedelic color on black, to moody organic gradients, to stark near-monochrome black/white line studies. Color often flows continuously across the surface like a dye or field rather than sitting in discrete regions.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Smooth, digital-clean vector-like flows; dense particle swarms; fine parallel curves that generate Moiré; fluid membrane/skin surfaces with additive luminosity and no grain — a slick, screen-native surface quality.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: "Almost meditative cumulative artworks" — swarms advect and self-organize, waveforms undulate, membranes breathe and morph slowly and continuously. The animation is organic flux without hard cuts; forms evolve as if alive, ideal for long ambient VJ passages.

- **Key works**: the numbered *dextro.org* algorithmic video series (e.g. 64, 65, 67B, 78); *turux.org* (interactive works with LIA); early 1990s net-art interactive pieces; the post-2002 high-res algorithmic video body of work.
