# Marius Watz

- **Bio/context**: Norwegian generative artist (b. 1973, Oslo; long based in NYC), one of the earliest and most influential figures in the field — active since the late 1990s. Codes in Processing/Java and founded the **Generator.x** platform/conference (2005) that helped name the "generative art" scene. Extends screen work into physical output: pen-plotter drawings, laser-cut panels, CNC, MakerBot 3D prints, and large architectural facades/projections.
- **Signature techniques**: Parametric form driven by mathematical fields. Core moves: (1) **Perlin/simplex-noise displacement of regular grids** ("Grid Distortion", "Modular Lattice", "BValSys"); (2) **polar arc-sweeps** subdivided into stacked concentric colored bands ("Arcs", "ArcSurf"); (3) **recursive extrusion** of a 2D footprint into 3D ribbon/columnar solids ("Extrusion", "RibForm", "Prime Hex"); (4) **probability-driven placement** ("Probability Lattice"). He frames this as deterministic system + bounded randomness = "software abstraction."
- **Palette logic**: Bold, saturated, high-contrast. Spectral **hue ramps mapped across a form** (color as a function of arc index or grid coordinate), often near-fluorescent RGB laid on flat black or white grounds. Plotter/print work drops to a limited flat-ink set; screen work embraces full-chroma gradients.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Crisp vector fills, hard edges, flat poster-like color planes with occasional thin outlines — screen-native, almost no grain. **Additive banding** where translucent arcs/ribbons overlap. In 3D-print work the "texture" is literal ridged extrusion geometry.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Most stills are frames of an animatable system — sweep the noise seed/phase and lattices breathe, arcs rotate, extrusions grow. **"ElectroPlastique"** is an explicit HD audio-reactive animation of pulsing generative form. For a VJ engine: drive noise time / arc phase / extrusion height off the beat so a static lattice becomes a slow morph or a pulsing bloom.
- **Key works**: *Arcs (Rockheim)* — building facade of stacked colored arc-bands; *ElectroPlastique #1* — audio-reactive HD generative animation; *Grid Distortion / Modular Lattice* — noise-warped module grids; *Probability Lattice* — stochastic 3D lattice; *Form Studies (MakerBot)* — 3D-printed generative solids; *ArcSurf* — arc-swept surface drawings.
