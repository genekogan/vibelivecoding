# Karsten Schmidt (toxi / PostSpectacular)

- **Bio/context**: German-born, London-based **computational designer**; studio **PostSpectacular**. Author of **toxiclibs** (2006, Processing/Java) and the sprawling **thi.ng** ecosystem (215+ TypeScript/Zig/GLSL/Clojure/C libraries) — a "build your own tools" open-source practitioner working across identity, product, data-viz, digital fabrication and generative art since ~2006. Teaches (CIID) and exhibits (V&A).
- **Signature techniques**: A tool-builder's toolkit. **VerletPhysics** particle/spring systems (`toxi.physics`) for form-finding; **volumetric iso-surface meshing / marching cubes** (`toxi.volume`) to grow smooth 3D-printable blobby geometry; **Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion** as surface pattern (the Print Magazine 2008 3D-printed cover); Voronoi/Delaunay, superellipse & polar geometry, L-systems, and WebGL/GLSL shader fields. **Parametric generative identity systems** where a logo/mark is a function re-run per instance rather than a fixed asset.
- **Palette logic**: Bright, systematic, code-defined color — programmatic gradient ramps and an HSV theme engine (`toxi.color` builds harmonious palettes from color-theory rules: complementary, split, analogous, tetrad), giving clean saturated computational gradients and controlled multi-hue schemes rather than photographic color.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Precise, vector-clean and geometric — smooth gradient meshes, crisp tessellations, flowing physics-relaxed curves, and modeled/3D-printed volumetric forms. Digital-fabrication finish (laser-cut, 3D-printed, plotted); little grain — clarity and structure over texture.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Physics-driven settling (verlet meshes relaxing to equilibrium), reaction-diffusion patterns spreading and breathing, and re-instantiable identities that regenerate per frame/use. Motion is systemic and continuous — well suited to parametric fields that morph smoothly on a control signal.
- **Key works**: **toxiclibs** & **thi.ng** (the tools/aesthetic themselves); V&A **Decode** open-source "Recode" identity (2009); **onedotzero** Adventures in Motion generative festival identity (2009); *Forever* installation w/ Universal Everything (V&A, 2008); Print Magazine reaction-diffusion 3D-printed cover (2008); recent thi.ng generative art (incl. fxhash releases).
