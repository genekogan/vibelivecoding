# Kazumasa Teshigawara (qubibi)

- **Bio/context**: Japanese digital artist, born Tokyo 1977, working under the label *qubibi* since 2006 (formerly a web designer). Origins in ActionScript 3 / Adobe Flash, later C#/Unity; commercially awarded (D&AD, Cannes). His fine-art generative research crystallized in 2010 with the *hello world* algorithm and continues to evolve from that single seed.

- **Signature techniques**: The core algorithm **determines borders/boundaries through color** — a single digital point traces a growing, largely self-avoiding path, and *mimizu* ("earthworm") lets that path grow into organic, writhing worm-like bodies. Outputs read as extremely dense concentric circular bands with radial symmetry and contour/cross-section mapping, producing pseudo-3D volumetric depth from purely 2D linework; the process deliberately balances "the clashing of rules and chaos."

- **Palette logic**: Grayscale/black foundation with selective vivid accents — burgundy, deep crimson, rose, red, teal, and yellow glinting against pure black backgrounds. Color functions as boundary marker, so hues appear as thin jewel-tone seams inside dense monochrome masses.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Thousands of fine computational strokes packed into volumetric ribboned/worm forms and concentric rings; hand-tuned, improvisational feel despite being code-driven — dense, tactile, almost woven ("The Worm Fabric").

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: The forms *dance* — paths grow and undulate in real time, borders propagate outward, and the worm bodies wriggle and breathe (his own phrase: "a dancing worm"). Motion is continuous organic growth/writhing rather than cut transitions, well suited to slow hypnotic evolution.

- **Key works**: *hello world* (2010, foundational algorithm); *mimizu* (earthworm path growth); *The Worm Fabric*; *fill* / *fillspector*; *drifter*; *daydream*; *diagolus*.
