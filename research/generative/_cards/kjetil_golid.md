# Kjetil Golid

- **Bio/context**: Norwegian creative coder and software engineer (Oslo). Runs the **generated.space** portfolio and co-authored the **generativeartistry.com** tutorial series; an OG Art Blocks curated artist. Works in JavaScript on Canvas/SVG (p5.js/paper.js), strongly palette-driven. Era ~2017–present.
- **Signature techniques**: Structured, diagrammatic geometric systems with circuit-board clarity. **Archetype**'s engine is a **recursive rectangle subdivision** (guillotine / slice-and-dice partition — every way to cut a rectangle into sub-rectangles), whose cells are filled from a small vocabulary of primitives: concentric arcs, nested rectangles, connective "pipes"/bezier links, and dot grids — so the canvas reads as abstract architecture or a schematic. Other systems across his catalog: **graph/network node-link layouts**, **flow-field line packing**, **tilings and truchet variants**, and **chromatic-aberration RGB channel offset**. Everything is deterministic and rule-based.
- **Palette logic**: Curated **named palettes** (he maintains a large palette collection, drawing on nice-color-palettes / cpt-city plus hand-tuned sets). Typically a tight 3–6 flats with one accent — warm neutrals or cream/black grounds with a pop hue, or bold primaries. An occasional **RGB split** adds cyan/magenta fringing at edges.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Flat vector fills, crisp geometric edges, thin consistent linework, often over a subtle paper-grain or faint grid backdrop. The deliberate chromatic-aberration offset lends a printed/misregistered feel. Minimal, editorial, poster-clean.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Outputs are static generative stills, but the primitive vocabulary is trivially animatable — **grow the subdivision cell-by-cell**, sweep arcs within their cells, pulse network edges, or drift the RGB channels apart and back. For a VJ engine: animate the rectangle partition assembling, arcs drawing on, and chromatic split breathing on the beat.
- **Key works**: *Archetype* (Art Blocks project #23, 2021) — rectangle-subdivision abstract "paintings," his signature series; *generativeartistry.com* tutorials (circle packing, hypnotic squares, joy division, etc.); tiling / graph / flow-field sketches on generated.space; various fxhash & objkt drops.
