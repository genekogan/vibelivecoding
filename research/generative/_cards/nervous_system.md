# Nervous System

- **Bio/context**: Design studio founded 2007 by **Jessica Rosenkrantz** (MIT biology + architecture, Harvard GSD) and **Jesse Louis-Rosenberg** (MIT math/CS). Based in Palenville/Catskills, NY. They write custom generative simulations (historically **Processing** and **Cinder/C++**, plus WebGL/JS web-apps) that drive **digital fabrication** — 3D printing (nylon SLS, resin), laser-cut wood/metal, jewelry, textiles, jigsaw puzzles. In MoMA, Cooper Hewitt, MFA Boston collections. Their looks are directly reproducible as p5 field/agent systems.

- **Signature techniques** (one algorithm per product line, all worth stealing):
  - **Hyphae / Xylem** — **space-colonization venation** (Runions et al. leaf-vein algorithm): auxin/attractor points pull a branching vascular network; edges thicken by Murray's-law flow accumulation → dense root/leaf/lung dendritic webs.
  - **Floraform** — **differential growth of an elastic surface**: a triangulated ribbon grows locally faster than it can stay flat, so it buckles into ruffled kale/coral frills; edges subdivide when stretched, vertices repel.
  - **Reaction (necklaces) / their RD imagery** — **Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion** and multi-scale Turing patterns (some commissioned from Jonathan McCabe).
  - **Kinematics** — rigid triangular/tessellated panels + hinges, **rigid-body-simulation folding** to compress a large form into a printable volume that springs open.
  - **Laplacian Growth / Dendrite / Radiolaria** — **DLA / Laplacian (∇²) growth** and Voronoi/Delaunay cellular subdivision (Radiolaria = Voronoi on a sphere).
  - **Cell Cycle** — polar/radial WebGL editor generating Voronoi-on-a-surface lattices of revolution.
  - Recent blog work: **physarum/slime-mold agent fields** (collab w/ Sage Jenson) and floral-tapestry generative textiles.

- **Palette logic**: Two registers. (1) **Material-honest** — the natural color of the fabricated stuff: bone nylon white, black steel, brass/gold, walnut, translucent resin, so *form and shadow* carry it. (2) **Screen/generative** — when they render the sims: soft biological gradients, tissue pinks and reds (lung/geode), oxidized teal-to-magenta, vein maps on cream. Generally tasteful, desaturated-organic, not neon.

- **Mark-making / texture**: **Line-network and cellular filigree** — thin branching veins, lace-like Voronoi cell walls, ruffled surface pleats. Crisp, precise, jewelry-fine. Even the RD/venation renders read as delicate ink-on-vellum botanical illustration rather than painterly.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: All their systems are **growth processes** and animate beautifully: veins *creep and branch* toward attractors (space colonization), the Floraform ribbon *ruffles and buckles* as it grows, RD spots *spread and split*, DLA fronts *accrete*. For a VJ loop: run space-colonization branching or differential-growth subdivision live, advancing the growth front on a slow clock, optionally seeded/steered by audio. Organic, ever-branching, monotonic growth (no hard loop).

- **Key works**: *Hyphae* (2009, venation lamps/jewelry); *Xylem* (venation app/prints); *Floraform* (2016, differential-growth jewelry & sculpture, STARTS Prize); *Kinematics* (2013, folding 3D-printed dresses, in MoMA); *Cell Cycle* (WebGL jewelry generator); *Reaction* (RD necklaces); *Radiolaria/Dendrite* (early Voronoi & DLA jewelry); *Puzzles* (generative jigsaw, "Infinite Galaxy").
