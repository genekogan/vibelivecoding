# Carsten Nicolai (Alva Noto)

- **Bio/context**: German artist and musician (b. 1965, Karl-Marx-Stadt/Chemnitz; based Berlin). Sound installations, sculpture, print, light, and AV performance; music alias **Alva Noto**, co-founder of the **raster-noton / raster-media** label. Active 1990s–present. His project is making the inaudible and invisible perceptible — *sound made visible* — via scientific, systemic methods (he insists the patterns are generated by **formula/code, not graphic-design software**).

- **Signature techniques**: **Cymatics** — sound/frequency vibrating liquids (milk, water) and particles into standing-wave patterns (*milch*, *wellenwanne*; *wellenwanne lfo* drives a water tank with low-frequency oscillators, filmed from above). **Moiré / interference** — overlaid line grids generating emergent patterns (*moiré rota/film/glas*; *grid index* catalogues thousands of grid patterns as a printed reference). **Grid systems, random-dot fields, error/glitch structures, self-organizing systems.** **Crystallography** (*einkristall*, *snow noise* — crystal symmetry). **Strobe/frequency light** (*α pulse*; *unidisplay*, a ~40 m interactive strobe/pattern mirror-wall). Modular color-field grids (*unicolor*). Scientific-instrument aesthetic (oscilloscope, test signal).

- **Palette logic**: Austere **near-monochrome** — black, white, grayscale — with disciplined single accents: **signal red**, occasionally **CMYK primaries** or a spectral band. *unicolor* is the exception, built from saturated modular color blocks. Milk-white on black; restraint is the governing rule.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Precise thin **gridlines and rasters**, halftone dot fields, **moiré shimmer**, liquid **cymatic ripples**, crisp geometric bars, high-contrast flat fields, and luminous strobe. Nothing painterly — every mark is mechanical/systemic.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Driven by **frequency/oscillation** — cymatic patterns morph as pitch sweeps; moiré shimmers as layers slide over one another; grids pulse and phase; strobe flickers at audio rate. **Slow parametric LFO sweeps vs. sharp beat-locked flashes**, tightly coupled to Alva Noto's sine tones and clicks so that sound and image read as the same signal.

- **Key works**: **milch** (2000, cymatic milk) · **wellenwanne / wellenwanne lfo** (2001/2012, water-tank cymatics) · **snow noise** (2001) · **syn chron** (2005, crystalline light architecture) · **moiré** series + **grid index** (2010–11, Gestalten book) · **unidisplay** (2012, 40 m strobe/pattern wall) · **particle noise** (2013) · **unicolor** (2014, modular color grids) · **α (alpha) pulse** (2014, strobe app).
