# Manolo Gamboa Naon

- **Bio/context**: Argentine artist/creative coder (b. ~1988, La Plata), known as **"Manoloide."** Widely regarded as one of generative art's great colorists. Works mainly in **Processing** (also openFrameworks, p5.js, Unity), grown out of a prolific daily-sketch practice on Instagram/OpenProcessing. Major presence on fxhash, Feral File, Verse, and Kate Vass Galerie. Era ~2015–present.
- **Signature techniques**: **Massive over-plotting** — thousands of simple primitives (short strokes, dots, arcs, triangles, bezier ribbons) accumulated with transparency to build painterly depth from cheap parts. Marks are oriented by **noise/flow fields**; per-layer positional **jitter and duplicated offset layers** create chromatic misregistration and glow. He downplays algorithmic novelty in favor of **composition of large color masses** — "the digital surface as plastic space, code as material." Recurring motifs: overlapping translucent blobs, gestural ribbon bundles, and grids where each cell is a miniature color study.
- **Palette logic**: The signature. Rich, surprising, **saturated multi-hue palettes** — deliberate warm/cool clashes that resolve into harmony, usually a dominant field vibrating against complementary accents. Hues are **optically mixed via layered transparency and additive/screen overlaps**, so colors blend at the edges rather than sitting flat. Hand-curated palettes, never default RGB.
- **Mark-making / texture**: Brushy, tactile, painterly despite being pure vector — achieved by stippled over-plotting, soft-edged marks, and overlap glow. Reads like **risograph / screenprint or oil impasto**; grain comes from density, not filters. Rarely flat or hard-edged.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Much output is still, but he animates — **slow drift of the underlying flow field**, marks streaming along vectors, palette cross-fades, loops where the mark-cloud accumulates then dissolves. For a VJ engine: advect his stroke-clouds along a slowly rotating noise field and cross-fade curated palettes across sections for that "painting assembling itself" feel.
- **Key works**: fxhash generative drops; *marmol*; his large OpenProcessing sketch corpus; Feral File / Verse collaborations; named studies (*arbolito*, *plasma*, *mantel*, *miedo*, *galpon*, *circles*). The through-line is the instantly recognizable "Manoloide look" of chaotic, luminous color.
