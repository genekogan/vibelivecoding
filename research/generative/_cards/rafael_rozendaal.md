# Rafaël Rozendaal

- **Bio/context**: Dutch-Brazilian artist (b. 1980), based between Amsterdam and New York. Since 2000 his primary medium is the **website-as-artwork**: dozens of individually-titled generative sites at poetic `.com` domains (built in HTML/JS **Canvas/WebGL**), each sold to a collector who must keep it publicly online. Collectively ~50M+ visits/year. Also makes lenticulars, **jacquard tapestries** (Abstract Browsing), haiku and installations. Exhibited at the Stedelijk, Museum Folkwang, and via Rhizome.
- **Signature techniques**: **minimal procedural animation loops** — a handful of flat shapes or gradients driven by simple continuous transforms (translate/scroll, scale, hue-cycle, sine oscillation) so the image **moves endlessly and never exactly repeats**. Many are **interactive**: a click or mouse-move flips state, changes color, or nudges the system. *Abstract Browsing* (2014) is a browser plug-in that renders any webpage as its **DOM skeleton of flat colored rectangles** — the structure of the web as generative composition.
- **Palette logic**: bold, **saturated, poster-flat** color — big hard-edge blocks of primaries and secondaries at high contrast; occasional smooth full-screen **gradient washes** (Falling Falling, Into Time). Palettes are punchy, graphic, and deliberately "cheap-web" cheerful; chosen for immediacy, not naturalism.
- **Mark-making / texture**: **flat hard-edge vector fills, zero grain or modeling** — pure crisp geometry (rectangles, circles, sweeping bands). The web-native aesthetic: clean, resolution-independent, screen-luminous.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: **infinite non-looping generative motion** — "the feeling of a waterfall or a fountain, endless and meaningless." Signature behaviors: perpetual vertical **gradient scroll** (*Falling Falling*), **unrolling** (*Paper Toilet*), binary **on/off** oscillation, slow drips and morphs, hue drift. Simple, hypnotic, continuous; user interaction perturbs but never stops the loop.
- **Key works**: *Falling Falling .com* (2011, endless downward gradient with sound); *Much Better Than This .com* (2006); *Paper Toilet .com* (2006, an interactive unrolling roll); *Stagnation Means Decline .com* (2002); *On And Off .org* (2003); *Into Time .com*; *Abstract Browsing* (2014, tapestry/plug-in series reducing sites to colored blocks); *Some Still .com* (2019).
