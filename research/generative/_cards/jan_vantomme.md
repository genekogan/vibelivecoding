# Jan Vantomme

- **Bio/context**: Belgian computational designer based in Ghent (studio *Vormplus*), co-founder of Processing Ghent (2011) and author of *Processing 2: Creative Programming Cookbook* (Packt, 2012). Builds custom software in Processing, openFrameworks and Cinder; his generative practice is collected at **generative.systems**. Active mid-2000s to present, rooted in the classic Processing/graphic-design lineage.

- **Signature techniques**: Grid-driven generative composition is the backbone — modular grids, triangle grids, and "grid composition studies" where each cell is seeded with a randomized primitive or rotation. Also does **reaction-diffusion** (his `rds` series = Gray-Scott), **metaballs** (implicit iso-surfaces / marching-squares thresholding), scribble/hatch fills that emulate pen-plotter line packing, and "brutalism" pieces built from heavy bisected rectangles. Compositions are usually deterministic-with-seed so a plotter or print can reproduce them.

- **Palette logic**: Restrained, print-designer palettes — often duotone or 3-4 flat spot colors on off-white/cream, riso-adjacent (warm red + blue/black). "Autumn"/"Summer" pieces swap to a small seasonal ramp. Rarely uses smooth photographic gradients; color is applied as flat fills per cell/region.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Flat vector fills and clean geometry dominate, contrasted against dense hand-drawn-looking **scribble and cross-hatch** textures (many short randomized strokes filling a shape) that give a plotter/ink feel. Hard edges, no glow; texture comes from line density, not blur.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Much of the archive reads as still/print output, but the systems animate naturally by advancing the seed or the RD simulation over time — reaction-diffusion blooms and spreads, metaballs slide and merge, scribble fills accrete stroke-by-stroke, grids can re-roll cell states on a beat. Good for slow morphing tiles and gradual fill-in reveals.

- **Key works**: *Reaction-Diffusion Studies (rds-1-1…)* — Gray-Scott coral/labyrinth fields; *Metaballs* — merging implicit blobs; *Modular / Triangle / Grid Composition Studies* — combinatorial cell grids; *Scribble Grid / Scribble Lines* — plotter-style hatch fills; *Brutalism 1 & 2* — bold bisected-rectangle geometry; *Autumn / Summer* — seasonal palette color-field pieces.
