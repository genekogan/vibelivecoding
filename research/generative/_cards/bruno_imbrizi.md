# Bruno Imbrizi

- **Bio/context**: Brazilian-born creative developer in London (ex-FIELD.IO), graphic-designer-turned-coder with 10+ years across agencies and brands (Dell, Adidas, Universal, Nike, Google Pixel). Works in JavaScript/WebGL/GLSL, three.js, OpenFrameworks/C++, and TouchDesigner; teaches the well-known Domestika "Creative Coding: Making Visuals with JavaScript" courses built on Matt DesLauriers' `canvas-sketch`. Publishes tutorials on Codrops and Generative Artistry.

- **Signature techniques**: **GPGPU particle systems** are his hallmark — image/text pixels sampled into an FBO (framebuffer) simulation where positions/velocities live in floating-point textures and are advected by curl/flow noise, with mouse-picking via an offscreen hit-pass pushing particles apart (his Codrops "Interactive Particles with Three.js"). Also flow fields ("Go With the Flow"), steering/flocking behaviors, grid systems, generative typography, and audio-reactive visualizers. Uses `simplex`/curl noise, instanced meshes, and custom GLSL displacement.

- **Palette logic**: Designer-clean and often brand-driven — controlled 2-3 color schemes, frequent duotone (e.g. Generative Artistry "Hours of Dark") and confident single-accent-on-dark. Comfortable with vivid saturated brand palettes and smooth GLSL gradients, but composition-first so color stays legible.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Points and thin lines rendered additively for luminous particle clouds; crisp instanced geometry; smooth shader gradients. Texture emerges from **density fields** of thousands-to-millions of GPU particles rather than grain or halftone — soft where diffuse, sharp where they resolve into an image or glyph.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Everything is physics/flow-driven and continuous — particles ease between a rest state (forming a logo/word/image) and a perturbed state, advected by noise and repelled by pointer interaction, then spring back. Strong sense of settle → disturb → reform loops, plus audio-reactive pulsing; ideal reference for interactive/beat-responsive particle scenes.

- **Key works**: *Interactive Particles with Three.js* (Codrops tutorial — image→FBO particle displacement); *Hours of Dark* (Generative Artistry — generative duotone poster); *Go With the Flow* (flow-field piece); brand installations for Dell, Adidas, Universal Music, Nike Rise; Domestika "Creative Coding 2.0: Animation, Sound & Color".
