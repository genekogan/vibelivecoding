# Ryoji Ikeda

- **Bio/context**: Japanese composer & visual artist (b. 1966; Paris/Kyoto), ex-**Dumb Type**. Makes large-scale audiovisual installations and concerts driven by **custom real-time software** (C/OpenGL, with collaborators Norimichi Hirakawa, Tomonaga Tokuyama, Shohei Matsukawa) where *every pixel is calculated by mathematical principle*. Active 1995–present. The defining aesthetic of "data as sublime."

- **Signature techniques**: **Data sonification/visualization.** *test pattern* converts arbitrary data (text, audio, image, video) into **black-and-white barcode / binary (0–1) patterns** scrolling at extreme frame rates (hundreds of fps, a stress-test for both projector and retina). *datamatics* renders scientific datasets — DNA sequences, particle-physics events, star maps, geomagnetic fields — as **flowing point/scatter fields, rotating 3D point clouds, and 4D→3D→2D hypercube projections** over wireframe grids. Recurring primitives: scrolling numeric/coordinate matrices (columns of digits & timestamps), oscilloscope **sine/waveform** traces, high-frequency line rasters that self-generate **moiré**. Extreme minimalism + strobe.

- **Palette logic**: **Near-pure 1-bit monochrome** — black and white, high contrast, with occasional single striking accents (a spectral color band, or pure grayscale). "Color" is really **data density**: white marks accumulate on black until the screen whites out. Works like *supersymmetry* and *micro | macro* are almost exclusively white-on-black.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Crisp **aliased** pixels and barcode bars, dense fields of tiny points and numerals, thin white gridlines, moiré shimmer from over-fine rasters — all **flat** (no gradients, no shading except data-driven). Flicker and strobe function as texture in themselves.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Ultra-fast horizontal **scrolling and flicker**; hard cuts between near-stillness and blizzards of data; slow majestic rotations of 3D point clouds alternating with seizure-fast strobe passages. Structured like music into sections — quiet numeric fields build to overwhelming data-storms — and **locked sample-tight to sine-tone/glitch-click audio** (sound and image are one signal).

- **Key works**: **test pattern** (2008–, data→barcode) · **datamatics** (2006–, incl. **data.tron**, **data.scan**, **data.matrix**, **data.path**, **data.anatomy**) · **the transfinite** (2011, Park Avenue Armory) · **supersymmetry** (2014, particle physics) · **superposition** (2012) · **micro | macro** (2015–) · **spectra** (vertical light-beam installations) · **data-verse** (2019, Fondazione Prada — cosmic→cellular scales).
