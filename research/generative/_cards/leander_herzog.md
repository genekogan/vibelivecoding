# Leander Herzog

- **Bio/context**: Swiss visual artist (b. 1984, based in Basel) making images with code since 2006; his practice is real-time generative and interactive abstraction that runs live in the browser. Medium is hand-written JavaScript — WebGL + GLSL for raster pieces, raw SVG for vector pieces, occasionally Web Audio — rather than a framework. A central figure of the on-chain generative era (fxhash, Art Blocks-adjacent, Verse); exhibited/collected at HEK Basel, ZKM Karlsruhe, Kunsthalle Zürich, and Francisco Carolinum Linz. His 2026 HEK show "runtime" displays one work per day, fullscreen, running in realtime and perpetually regenerating.

- **Signature techniques**: Extreme minimalism built from a single deterministic algorithm per piece — recursive subdivision of the plane, grid deformation, folding/creasing of quad meshes, circle/rect packing, and vector-field line integration rendered as SVG paths. Many pieces are seed-driven parameter spaces (mint a token → a point in the space) that evolve in real time rather than rendering one frozen image. "Heatsink" reads as thermal fin/diffusion fields; "Flips" and "fold" are origami-like planar creasing; "Infinite Garden" and "Alp" are procedural minimal landscapes from layered horizon bands and noise.

- **Palette logic**: Reductive. Frequently pure black-on-white or white-on-black with a single saturated accent (primary red/blue/yellow), or a tight thermal/heatmap ramp (deep blue → magenta → orange) for the Heatsink family. Flat digital color, no photographic texture; when color appears it is bold and few (2–4 hues), used as information not decoration.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Hard-edge and clean — crisp anti-aliased WebGL fills or exact SVG vector strokes, zero grain, zero painterly modeling. Surfaces are flat planes of color separated by precise contours; the "texture" is entirely structural (density of subdivision, line spacing, fold count).

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: Perpetual real-time self-generation — the algorithm never stops, drifting/morphing/folding slowly and deterministically, sometimes driven by mouse or audio interaction (e.g. the SVG piece "Returns" is interactive across its 500 editions). No pre-rendered loops; the temporal signature is patient, continuous transformation of a live geometric system.

- **Key works**: *Alp* (150 minimalist abstract Swiss landscapes); *Returns* (500 interactive SVG pieces on fxhash); *Heatsink* (thermal fin/diffusion fields, editions #65–67); *Flips* / *fold* (planar creasing / origami folding); *Infinite Garden* (procedural minimal gardens, ig760–762); *Rain*, *Bank*, *Dancefloor*, *Minmax*, *Strich* (single geometric studies).
