# Pascal Dombis

- **Bio/context**: French artist (b. 1965, Metz), based in Paris; engineering degree from INSA Lyon before turning self-taught to algorithmic art in the early 1990s. Medium: **algorithmic prints, lenticular panels, and large-scale wall/floor installations** driven by custom software (he writes the generative rules, then outputs to print/lenticular/LED). ~30-year career; shown at the Moscow Biennale, DAN Galeria, Bluerider ART, Art Plural.
- **Signature techniques**: **excessive recursive repetition of a single simple process** — he lays down one trivial rule (a short line, a curve, a glyph, a typographic sign) and iterates it to abusive excess until emergent, unplanned complexity appears. The signature *Irrational Geometrics* are dense superimpositions of **many families of stripes/curves at slightly different angles and phases**, producing large-scale **moiré / interference** fields. Later text works (*The Ends of Painting/Words*, *The Invisible Generation*, *I Am Not a Robot*) run the same proliferation logic on letterforms and CAPTCHA fragments.
- **Palette logic**: **stochastic, non-designed color** — layered coloured *and* black-and-white line families whose overlaps produce accidental chromatic beats; often high-contrast with saturated primaries/secondaries riding over black/white substrates; the lenticular adds angle-dependent **chromatic shift/aberration**. Color is a by-product of the flicker, not a chosen scheme.
- **Mark-making / texture**: thousands of thin vector strokes of varying weight, laid in overlapping rotated grids; the emergent texture is pure **moiré interference** — shimmering, unstable, glossy on the lenticular surface. Crisp mechanical linework, no hand-grain; density gradients read as tonal cloud.
- **Motion/temporal qualities**: the prints are static but **engineered for temporal shimmer** — lenticular parallax means the image *flickers, flows and re-forms as the viewer moves* (angle of incidence filters between layered images/texts), simulating "an uninterrupted flow of information." For an animated port: slowly **rotate/translate overlapping line families** to sweep the moiré through the frame; cross-fade layered text.
- **Key works**: *Irrational Geometrics* (2000s–, lenticular stripe/curve interference — his signature); *The Ends of Painting* (2019, drips/Pollock-referencing line excess); *Remix / The Ends of Words* (2021–24, typographic proliferation); *The Invisible Generation* (Burroughs cut-up text fields); *Post-Digital Mirror* (2013); *I Am Not a Robot* (2021, recursive CAPTCHA); *Hot Dark Future / Post-Digital Surface*; *Spin Machine* (2024); *Chromatic Arc / Aurora* (2024–26, arc gradients).
