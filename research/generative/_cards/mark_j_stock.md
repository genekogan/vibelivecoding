# Mark J. Stock

- **Bio/context**: American generative artist, scientist and programmer (PhD in aerospace/mechanical engineering, computational fluid dynamics). Since the early 2000s he writes his own high-performance CFD solvers (C++/CUDA, open-source vortex codes like **Omega2D/Omega3D**) and freezes single frames of the simulation as large archival prints, acrylic face-mounts and SLS/foam 3D-printed sculpture. Based in Boston (Fort Point). Straddles the sci-viz / fine-art line.

- **Signature techniques**: **Vortex-particle / vortex-method fluid dynamics** (Lagrangian vorticity, not Eulerian grids) — he simulates buoyant plumes, mixing layers, and the **collision of fluid droplets** (Immaculate Collision) at enormous particle counts, then renders density as if X-rayed. Also **diffusion-limited aggregation (DLA)** and dielectric-breakdown branching (Dendrite series: twigs, sprawl, lightning-like arbors), and **Voronoi/nearest-neighbour color-exchange** cellular automata (Field series). Everything is a *classical physics simulation* pushed to "trillions of operations," captured mid-turbulence.

- **Palette logic**: Painterly and moody, keyed to the phenomenon: **magma reds/oranges over black**, ink-in-water blues and teals, smoke greys, occasional bright chartreuse tendrils on dark. Often **additive/emissive density** (bright where fluid piles up, black where sparse) giving a luminous, X-ray or nebula quality. High dynamic range, deep blacks, one or two dominant hues per piece — rarely a full rainbow.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Wispy, filamentary, **smoke-and-marble** texture from the vortex filaments — fine striations, feathered edges, fractal self-similar curls (Kelvin-Helmholtz roll-ups, mushroom caps). Dendrite works are crisp branching line. Reads as photographic/volumetric (soft, continuous tone) rather than flat vector.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: The prints are frozen instants, but the underlying process is **advection + roll-up + diffusion**: dye stretched and folded by a velocity field, vortices spinning off smaller vortices, plumes mushrooming. For a VJ loop: curl-noise or stable-fluids dye advection with buoyancy, emissive accumulation, slow bloom outward; dendrite variants = a DLA/breakdown front creeping across the frame. Turbulent-but-slow, continuously curling, never looping cleanly.

- **Key works**: *Immaculate Collision* (2014–17, X-ray droplet-collision fluid); *COSM* (fluid-dynamics canvases, 40"×40"); *Gyre* (ocean-eddy vortex fields); *Field* (Voronoi color-exchange CA); *Particle / Diaspora* (self-organizing vortex particle systems); *Dendrite* (DLA branching — *Sprawl*, *Twigs*); *Triple Fluid Collision* (SLS 3D-printed turbulent sculpture).
