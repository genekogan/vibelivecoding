# Reza Ali

- **Bio/context**: Computational designer, software engineer and visual/spatial artist (San Francisco Bay Area; site "syedrezaali.com"). Works across **openFrameworks / Cinder / C++**, Processing, WebGL/p5, and now **Apple Metal** (his own `Satin` framework and `Valence` 3D modeling app). Ex-Google Daydream UX engineer, Autodesk Pier 9 artist-in-residence, Skycatch drone software; deeply focused on computational geometry, generative design and building his own creative tools.

- **Signature techniques**: Real-time **particle + spring physics** systems; a C++ **fluid solver** (Navier–Stokes) whose velocity field warps particle/spring terrains (*FluidScapes* — "making the invisible visible"). **Superformula / supershapes** for 2D & 3D parametric form; **Lorenz attractor** particle clouds (*Lormalized*, ~25k particles); **signed distance functions** live-coded into 3D form (*F3 – Form From Function*, exportable for 3D print); **generative typography** where letterforms become particle/spring fields (*mis.shap.en.ness*); Voronoi tessellation; and audio-reactive VJ engines. A tool-builder as much as an artist (`ofxUI`, `Satin`, `Valence`).

- **Palette logic**: Two modes. His generative/AV work uses **vibrant spectral gradients and neon-on-black** — iridescent hue ramps mapped to velocity, depth or audio amplitude. His design-tool and later Metal work uses a clean, bright, white-background aesthetic with physically-shaded (PBR) materials and restrained accent color.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Fine **additive** lines, points and particle trails with glow in the AV pieces; springy wireframe meshes; and smooth GPU-shaded 3D surfaces (Metal PBR) in the tool/sculpture work. Ranges from delicate luminous linework to solid modeled form.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: **Audio-reactive** pulsing and hue shifts; fluid advection continuously warping terrains; particle systems settling and jittering under spring forces; live-coded parameters morphing form in real time; smooth camera orbits around evolving 3D geometry. Motion is either driven by sound or by an ongoing simulation.

- **Key works**: *FluidScapes* (fluid field × particle/spring terrain); *Lormalized* (Lorenz-attractor particle cloud); *2D/3D SuperShapes* (superformula); *mis.shap.en.ness* (generative typography); *F3 – Form From Function* (SDF live-coding); *POSSIBLE* (Deadmau5 audio-visual VJ app); *Voronoi Puzzles* (Autodesk Pier 9); tools *ofxUI*, *Satin*, *Valence 3D*.
