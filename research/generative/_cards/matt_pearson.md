# Matt Pearson

- **Bio/context**: UK artist-coder (Brighton), blogger at zenbullets.com, and author of *Generative Art: A Practical Guide Using Processing* (Manning, 2011) — one of the formative English-language texts on the craft. Best known for the *100 Abandoned Artworks* project (abandonedart.org, now defunct — mirrored on the Wayback Machine), a sketch-a-day archive of ephemeral Processing pieces. Era ~2008–2013, the Processing "golden age."

- **Signature techniques**: The Manning book's chapters *are* his toolkit: Perlin noise fields, randomness/emergence, fractals, agent-oriented programming (boids/flocking — see `kaleidoboids`, kaleidoscope + flock), cellular automata, 3D drawing, and Lissajous curves. His signature look is **layering thousands of thin, low-alpha lines and curves** to build dense soft texture ("skinny lines"). `Unknown Pleasures` recreates the Joy Division ridgeline (stacked noise-perturbed horizontal traces); `PetriDish` reads as reaction-diffusion-like organic growth; `Interference` is line Moiré.

- **Palette logic**: Frequently near-monochrome — grayscale or a single hue with low-opacity strokes that accumulate into smoke-like gradients. Occasional restrained tri-color schemes (his `tricolpalette`). Inky, subdued, atmospheric rather than saturated.

- **Mark-making / texture**: Fine translucent lines and curves stacked additively; blur, smoke, and ribbon textures built from motion trails; no solid fills — everything is the sum of many faint strokes, giving a pencil/charcoal softness.

- **Motion/temporal qualities**: These were living sketches — noise-driven drift, particle advection with accumulating trails, boid flocking, and Lissajous oscillation. The aesthetic *is* temporal accumulation: forms emerge frame-by-frame as strokes pile up, then dissolve — perfect for continuous evolving VJ loops.

- **Key works**: *005 Spines*; *007 Interference* (line Moiré); *009 Loom* (woven lines); *012 Kaleidoboids* (kaleidoscopic flocking); *116 PetriDish* (organic growth); *118 Lissajous Blur*; *126/127 Natzke/Ribbon Smoke* (advected ribbons); *128 Unknown Pleasures* (Joy Division ridgeline).
