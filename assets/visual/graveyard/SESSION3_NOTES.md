# Wave-2 (2026-07-17) graveyard notes — twice-failed assets

Each was authored, deployed to the live canvas, judged on its pixels, requeued
with a specific written critique, rewritten — and still failed. Per the Ralph-loop
doctrine they are retired here rather than shipped. All four share one root cause,
which is worth reading before re-attempting them:

**They are all CONVERGENT or SPARSE botanical/fracture pieces.**

- `genart.tendril_climb` — motion 0.0038 → 0.0037 after rescue. The vine climbs and
  then stops; the frame is identical 8 s later. The rescue added circumnutation but
  the structure still settles. A working version needs growth *balanced by* death,
  not growth plus a wiggle.
- `genart.petal_unfurl` — motion 0.0052 → 0.006. Same convergence: the bloom opens
  and is done. Needs a staggered bud→unfurl→senesce→drop cycle across many blooms so
  something is always mid-transition, and far more of them.
- `genart.leaf_margin` — motion 0.0155, lum 0.533. Sparse blobs on cream; the
  morphogenesis never reads. Needs ONE frame-filling specimen (a botanical plate),
  not several small ones.
- `genart.safety_glass` — lum 0.19, reads as two soft blue blobs. The dicing
  fracture network never materialised. Needs a genuine dense frame-filling web with
  lit crack edges.

**The general lesson** (now in `scratchpad/gf/w3/W3_ADDENDUM.md`): a growth or
fracture process that *finishes* cannot pass a gate that compares frames 8 s apart.
Design perpetual process, or don't author the piece.
